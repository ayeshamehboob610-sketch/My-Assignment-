About University

The University of Layyah is an institution dedicated to providing quality higher education and creating a supportive academic environment for students.


The university focuses on academic excellence, research, innovation and practical learning. Students are encouraged to develop their knowledge, creativity and professional skills.


Through dedicated faculty, modern facilities and a student-focused environment, the University of Layyah aims to contribute to the educational and social development of the region.

University Library

📚 University Library

The University Library provides a peaceful and supportive environment for students, teachers and researchers.


Students can use the library for reading, assignments, research, academic projects and examination preparation. It provides an important space for learning and knowledge development.
